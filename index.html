Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Terafy ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the use of our mobile application (the "App").

1. Overview

Terafy does not directly collect, store, or process personal data on its own servers.

The App stores limited gameplay information locally on the user’s device and integrates third-party SDKs that may collect certain data automatically as described below.

2. Information Stored Locally on the Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Tutorial completion status
  • Player progress and levels

This information:

  • Is stored exclusively on the user’s device
  • Is not transmitted to Terafy servers
  • Is not accessible by Terafy

Removing the App may delete this locally stored data.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Automatic Data Collection

3.1 Google Play Services

The App is distributed through Google Play and may use Google Play Services for platform functionality, security, and performance.

Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Diagnostic and crash data
  • Usage data
  • IP address
  • App performance information

Data collection and processing by Google are governed by Google’s own Privacy Policy.

3.2 Unity Ads

The App uses advertising services provided by Unity Ads.

Unity Ads may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • IP address
  • Interaction data related to advertisements
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

This information may be used for:

  • Delivering contextual or personalized advertisements
  • Measuring advertisement performance
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Improving advertising services

Terafy does not control the data collected by Unity Ads and does not receive personal user data from Unity.

Users can manage advertising preferences through their device settings.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

Information collected by third-party services may be processed for:

  • App functionality and distribution
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Advertising delivery and measurement
  • Performance monitoring and improvement

Terafy does not sell personal data.

5. Data Sharing

Terafy does not share user data with third parties, except as required for the operation of integrated SDKs described in this Policy.

Third-party providers process data independently under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Terafy does not retain personal data on its own servers.

Third-party SDK providers may retain data in accordance with their respective privacy policies and legal obligations.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including:

  • Access to personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing

Requests concerning data collected by third-party SDKs should be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of the App. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
